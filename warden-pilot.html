Introducing the proposed Warden pilot programme

We are preparing a creator-led pilot programme for Warden, supported by the Virtual Silk Road and DigitalMe as default parts of the proposed entry experience. This is an invitation to learn about an emerging programme, not a claim that Warden is a generally available finished product.

Preview only — recruitment and applications are not yet open.

The official application destination, privacy notice, eligibility criteria and pilot participation agreement have not been approved. No application data is collected on this page, and no pilot licence or authority is granted.

Who is it for?

Marketing agencies, consulting firms, technology practitioners and qualified services companies interested in helping deliver a bounded Warden-enabled service pilot. Each selected pilot company would contract directly with, serve and bill its own customer under separately approved terms.

What happens to product rights?

Creators Common records eligible creator participation and attribution. Esomoire is proposed to maintain programme registry, marketplace listing and booking references, without automatically owning Warden, distributing licences, billing customers or controlling pilot transactions. Any creator compensation and subsequent general product licences require separate documented rights and commercial approval.

How the technology may be produced

Applications can describe Genesis or non-Genesis technology, each with Commons or external production. Additional third-party contributions are identified separately. All execution requires task-scoped consent and Warden authorization; River records the permitted evidence.

What is separate?

Quantum Arc is a distinct future product. Quantum Room is device-dependent and will not be commercially launched without a validated physical device.

Participation next steps

We will announce the official intake route and terms once approved. WhatsApp can be used for opt-in programme updates and invitations, but joining a group does not constitute pilot admission or licence purchase.
